AI Call Recovery

Missed call follow-up and appointment assistance by SMS.

Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions

What This Service Does

AI Call Recovery sends a follow-up SMS when a call is missed, unanswered, or disconnected quickly. The message is related only to the customer's recent call and is intended to help with service inquiries, customer support, or appointment scheduling.

SMS Program Description

This SMS program sends missed call follow-up messages related to service inquiries and appointment assistance. Messages are conversational, customer support related, and not promotional.

Consent / Opt-In

End users consent by calling the phone number associated with this service to request assistance, service information, or appointment scheduling. If the call is not answered, missed, or disconnected quickly, the system may send one follow-up SMS related only to that recent call.

STOP to opt out

Opt-Out

Users may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.

HELP for support

Help

Users may reply HELP for assistance.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies. Typically, one message is sent after a missed or unanswered call.

Rates

Message and data rates may apply.
